# Career Growth

**A Structured Guide to Career Paths and Real-World Opportunities**

---

## Overview

Career growth is not random.

It follows a structure:

**Skills → Projects → Portfolio → Visibility → Opportunities**

This guide shows how to move through each stage and turn learning into real-world outcomes.

---

## What You’ll Learn

- How to build practical skills  
- How to apply them through projects  
- How to present your work clearly  
- How to make your work visible  
- How visibility leads to opportunities  

---

## Key Idea

> Opportunities come from **visible, applied work**, not skills alone.

---

## Where to Show Your Work

- GitHub  
- LinkedIn  
- Personal portfolio (optional)  

Examples: repositories, portfolio sites, Streamlit apps
